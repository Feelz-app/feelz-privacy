Feelz Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

Feelz ("we," "our," or "us") provides a companion mobile gateway and related services. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: email address, username, and login metadata if you create an account.
  • Eligibility Data: age-confirmation status and related compliance confirmations.
  • Device and Usage Data: device type, OS version, app version, language, crash logs, and diagnostic events.
  • Support Communications: information you send when contacting support.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the Feelz service.
  • Operate safety, moderation, and legal-compliance controls.
  • Improve reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • Respond to customer support and legal requests.

3. Legal Bases and Eligibility

Feelz is intended for adults 18+. By using the service, you confirm you are at least 18 years old. We process information as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, and protect users.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with:

  • Hosting, infrastructure, analytics, and authentication service providers.
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or safety necessity.
  • Successors in the event of merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for service operation, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and security enforcement. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirements.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data.

  • To request access, correction, or deletion, contact us at privacy@feelz.app.
  • You may also request account deletion through support.

8. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we apply safeguards appropriate to cross-border data transfers.

9. Children’s Privacy

Feelz is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@feelz.app
